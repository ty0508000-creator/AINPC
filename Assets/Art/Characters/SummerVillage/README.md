# Summer Village characters

Art by **schwarnhild**. Source: https://schwarnhild.itch.io/summer-village
Downloaded 2026-10-13 from the free standard `summer_village_v1.0.zip`.

Includes old man (30×48 frame; four idle directions) and vegetable merchant (30×30 frame; three idle directions). Both have four-frame idle strips. Old man walking frames remain in the original sheet but current residents stay in place.

Used in the game's Stage1 village. Credit schwarnhild in game/release credits. Commercial and non-commercial project use and modification are allowed; asset resale or redistribution is prohibited. See the original `license.txt`. Do not distribute these files as a standalone asset pack.

Only character sheets were imported; the existing village terrain and houses are preserved. No recoloring or pixel editing was performed.
